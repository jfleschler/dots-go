Support / help and contact

Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email jon.fleschler@gmail.com with your device and what happened. Screenshots help.

New to Go?

Open Learn to Play. The dojo teaches the whole game on the board in short lessons, then coaches you through a real game against the AI.

How does a game end?

When neither side can gain anything, both players pass. Review opens with the stones that look dead already crossed out. Tap Done to count, or tap a group to change its mark. Against the AI, if you mark one of its living groups, it sends back a correction instead of playing on. If you disagree, Resume Play settles it on the board.

The who's-ahead meter

The bar under the score estimates who is leading. Tap it to see the territory each side can expect. It is an estimate, not a rule; turn it off in Settings › Board.

Daily puzzle and streaks

A new puzzle arrives each day, easiest on Monday and hardest at the weekend. Solving it on its day builds your streak. Missed a day? Play it from Past puzzles in the puzzle menu; it fills the week but doesn't count toward the streak. Reminders are optional, in Settings › Daily puzzle.

Online play and achievements

Play Online uses Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app if DOTS can't. Achievements and the streak leaderboard are sent once you are signed in.

Starting over

Settings › Reset clears lesson progress or preferences. Your saved games and history are kept.
