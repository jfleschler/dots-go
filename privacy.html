Privacy policy

DOTS: Go Rewired ("DOTS") is made by Jon Fleschler. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. The short version: DOTS does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your games, game history, lesson and daily-puzzle progress, streaks, achievements earned and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Online play, achievements and the daily-streak leaderboard use Apple's Game Center, and only if you are signed in to it. Apple runs Game Center and handles that information under Apple's privacy policy. Online matches carry the moves of the game. Opponents see your Game Center name, as in any Game Center game. We do not receive your Apple ID, email address or contact details.

Notifications

Daily puzzle reminders are optional. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Sharing

A game replay is made into an animated image on your device and is shared only when you choose to share it, through the share sheet, with whoever you pick.

Children

DOTS does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: jon.fleschler@gmail.com.
