DOTS is Go for people who have never played it. Learn on the real board, one move at a time, then play the offline AI, a friend on the same phone, or anyone through Game Center.

  • Learn by playing. Nineteen lessons on the board itself, from your first stone to a full game.
  • A daily duel. A small board halfway through a game, 5 × 5 on Monday up to 7 × 7 at the weekend. Win it to keep your streak.
  • An honest scorekeeper. Dead stones are marked for you at the end, and a meter shows who's ahead.
  • Replays. Step through any finished game and share it as an animated GIF.
  • 9 × 9, 13 × 13 and 19 × 19. Offline, two players on one device, or online.
